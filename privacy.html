Privacy Policy

GovWin OS — a service of The Feet Washers, Inc., Lauderdale Lakes, Florida. Last updated: November 6, 2026.

What we collect

Account information: your name, email address, company name, role, and state when you sign up. Workspace information: the business data you enter or upload — company profile, bids you track, quotes, documents, grant tracking, and messages you send through the platform (including questions to our AI guide and advisor requests). Free vendor listings: the business and skills information suppliers, subcontractors, and skilled professionals choose to publish so buyers can find them. Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; Stripe gives us your subscription status so your access follows your plan.

How we use it

To run your workspace and match opportunities to your profile; to show your vendor listing to buyers inside the platform (if you registered as a vendor); to answer support and advisor requests; to send service messages about your account; and to improve the platform. Opportunity, award, and grant data shown in GovWin comes from public sources (SAM.gov, Grants.gov, USAspending) and from what members enter themselves.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your private workspace data — your bids, quotes, uploads, or prices — with other members. Vendor listings show only the business information the vendor chose to publish; vendor documents (such as W-9 or insurance files) are never shown to other members.

AI guide

Questions you ask the in-app AI guide are processed to generate an answer from GovWin's own materials and your workspace data. Guide conversations are stored in your private workspace so you can refer back to them.

Cookies and advertising

This website uses only the cookies needed to run it. If advertising (such as Google AdSense) is enabled on our public guide pages, Google may use cookies to serve and measure those ads; you can control ad personalization in your Google account settings. Ads never appear inside member workspaces.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account information at any time by emailing nipteiapp@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your workspace data; records we must keep for payment and tax compliance are retained as the law requires.
