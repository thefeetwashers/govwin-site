Questions

Is SAM.gov registration really free?

Yes. Registering your entity on SAM.gov costs nothing at sam.gov. Our $399 service is paid help with the setup — only buy it if you want the help.

Do you take a percentage when I win a contract?

Never. GovWin charges flat plan and service fees only. No contingent percentage of any contract — that is a hard rule on this platform.

Which levels of government does GovWin cover?

Federal (live SAM.gov feed), plus state, county, city, school and special districts, cooperatives, and tribal & territories through playbooks, verified portal links, and tracked sources for all 50 states, DC, and the U.S. territories.

Is my company's information private?

Yes. Every company gets its own logon and workspace, and workspaces are isolated — your bids, quotes, uploads, and orders are not visible to other companies. Isolation was verified by direct cross-account testing.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan starts with a 30-day free trial with full features. A card is required to start the trial in Stripe; if you do not cancel before day 30, your subscription begins automatically and your card is charged. Cancel anytime before then and you are charged nothing. Your bids, quotes, and records stay in your workspace.

Can I cancel a plan?

Yes — plans are managed through Stripe. Contact us at nipteiapp@gmail.com and we will take care of it.
