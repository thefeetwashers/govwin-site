What GovWin OS is

GovWin OS is a private software platform that helps businesses find and pursue government contract and grant opportunities. It is not a government agency, and it is not affiliated with SAM.gov, Grants.gov, USAspending, or any government body. Opportunity data comes from public sources and member-entered information; always confirm requirements and deadlines in the official solicitation before you bid.

Plans and free trials

Paid plans (Finder, Bidder, Prime Team) are billed through Stripe. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial with full features of the chosen plan. A card is required to start the trial; if you do not cancel before day 30, the subscription begins automatically and the card is charged. You may upgrade, downgrade, or cancel through the Stripe billing portal; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period. GovWin charges flat plan and service fees only — never a percentage of any contract you win.

Refunds

Every plan begins with a 30-day free trial so you can evaluate GovWin OS before paying anything. Once a payment is made, it is non-refundable — this applies to subscription charges after the trial and to fixed-fee services once preparation work has begun. If you cancel a subscription, you keep full access until the end of the billing period already paid for.

Services

Fixed-fee services (such as SAM.gov Setup and 501(c)(3) Preparation) are help and preparation services. SAM.gov registration itself is free at sam.gov. 501(c)(3) preparation excludes the IRS filing fee and any state fees, is preparation help only, is not legal or tax advice, and does not guarantee approval by any agency.

Free vendor registration

Suppliers, subcontractors, service companies, and skilled professionals may register a business listing free of charge. Listings begin unverified; certifications and credentials in a listing are shown as self-reported until our team reviews them. A listing does not guarantee quote requests or work. Vendors contract with, and are paid by, the buyers they work with — GovWin OS is the meeting place, not a party to those contracts and not the payer.

No guarantee of results

GovWin provides tools, data, and guidance. We do not guarantee that you will win any contract or grant, and scores, matches, and pricing context in the platform are decision support — not predictions or promises. The AI guide answers from GovWin's materials and your data and can be wrong; verify bid-critical facts in the official solicitation.

Acceptable use

Use the platform lawfully and honestly: accurate listings and profiles, no forged quotes or credentials, no attempts to access another company's workspace. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms.

This website — including its opportunity listings, guides, and other content — is published for human visitors to read and use in their browsers. Automated scraping, bulk downloading, or bulk copying of the listings or guides on this site is not permitted.
