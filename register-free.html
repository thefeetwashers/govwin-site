A listing built around your skills

Your business name, your trades and skills, your NAICS codes, the products you supply, and the area you serve — written the way a prime searches when a deadline is close and a line item needs a price.

Found by primes who are bidding

Prime contractors search the GovWin directory and the supplier and subcontractor finder by skill, trade, code, and location. A complete listing surfaces when your work matches what a bid needs.

Quote requests in your own inbox

When a prime wants your price, the request lands in your GovWin inbox with the details of the work. You answer with your own price — your response is the quote the prime prices from. Nobody invents a price for you, and nobody edits yours.

Documents on file, labeled honestly

Who this is for

Three kinds of registrants make the directory work. If one of these sounds like you, the listing is free and takes minutes to start.

Suppliers & distributors

You sell the products government work consumes — materials, equipment, supplies, parts. Primes building product bids need firm supplier pricing; your listing puts your catalog categories and delivery area in front of them while the bid is still being priced.

Subcontractors & service companies

You perform part of the work a prime wins — cleaning, landscaping, trades, hauling, staffing, specialty services. Primes search by exact service and location; your listing tells them what you cover, where, and how to ask you for a number.

Skilled individuals

You bring a trade, a license, or hard-to-find experience as an independent professional. Register your skills and service area so companies assembling teams for government work can find the person they are missing.

How it works

Three steps, and only the first one is required to be found.

1. Register free.
Create your account with your name, email, and a password, and tell us whether you are a supplier, a subcontractor or service company, or a skilled individual. No card is asked for — there is nothing to buy.

2. Complete your listing.
Add your skills and trades, your NAICS codes, the products or services you offer, and the area you serve. The more complete the listing, the more searches it matches — and your capability profile is generated from it automatically.

3. Answer quote requests.
When a prime sends you a quote request, it arrives in your inbox with the scope and the deadline. Reply with your price and your terms. If the prime wins and awards you the work, you contract and invoice the prime directly.

The honest notes, up front: every listing starts out unverified, and any certifications you list are shown as self-reported until our team has reviewed supporting documents — we will not dress up an unchecked claim, including yours. Registering is always free, and GovWin never takes a percentage of your quotes or of any work you win through a connection made here. We also cannot promise you will receive requests: that depends on primes bidding work that matches your skills and area. A complete, accurate listing is what puts you in those searches.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Registering your business or your skills on GovWin OS costs nothing, and the free account does not expire. GovWin makes its money from the prime contractors who subscribe to find, price, and manage bids — not from the suppliers and subcontractors they need to find.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Free supplier, subcontractor, and skilled-professional registration never asks for a card, is not a trial, and never converts into a paid plan. There is nothing to cancel.

Who sees my listing?

Prime contractors and other businesses on GovWin who are searching the directory for suppliers, subcontractors, and skilled help for real bids. Your listing shows your skills, trades, NAICS codes, and service area. Listings start unverified, and certifications are shown as self-reported until they have been reviewed.

How do I get paid for work?

Directly from the prime contractor you agree to work with. You quote your price, you contract with the prime, and you invoice the prime — GovWin is the meeting place, not the payer, and it never takes a percentage of your quotes.
